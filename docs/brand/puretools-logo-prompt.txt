Color palette: solid deep indigo #4F46E5, near-white #FAFAFA, and one small electric-cyan #22D3EE accent only; high contrast; no additional colors
Materials/textures: completely flat solid fills with clean antialiased edges

Constraints: square 1:1 composition; original and ownable visual identity; favicon-ready; balanced negative space; no element may touch the canvas edge; keep all geometry simple enough for future vector tracing
